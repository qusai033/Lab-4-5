*IMPORTANT*

When a valid RFID tag is scanned, the door unlocks using the servo motor, the green LED turns on, and the PIR motion sensor and buzzer become active. 
If motion is detected while the door is unlocked, the buzzer will be activated. 
After a specified duration, the door will lock again, the green LED will turn off, and the PIR motion sensor and buzzer will become inactive.



Connections:

PIR motion sensor: Connect the sensor's VCC, GND, and OUT pins to the Arduino's 5V, GND, and digital pin 2, respectively.

RC522 RFID module: Follow the connections mentioned in the RFID module integration example.

Servo motor: Connect the servo's signal, VCC, and GND pins to the Arduino's digital pin 9, 5V, and GND, respectively.

General indication LED: Connect the LED's anode to the Arduino's digital pin 13 and the cathode to GND through a 220-ohm resistor.

Green LED for successful door unlock: Connect the LED's anode to the Arduino's digital pin 4 and the cathode to GND through a 220-ohm resistor.

Buzzer: Connect the buzzer's positive and negative pins to the Arduino's digital pin 3 and GND, respectively.


Step 1: Connect the RC522 RFID module to the Arduino board

Connect the SDA pin of the RC522 to the digital pin 10 on the Arduino board.
Connect the SCK pin of the RC522 to the digital pin 13 on the Arduino board.
Connect the MOSI pin of the RC522 to the digital pin 11 on the Arduino board.
Connect the MISO pin of the RC522 to the digital pin 12 on the Arduino board.
Connect the GND pin of the RC522 to a GND pin on the Arduino board.
Connect the RST pin of the RC522 to the digital pin 9 on the Arduino board.
Connect the 3.3V pin of the RC522 to the 3.3V pin on the Arduino board.
Step 2: Install the MFRC522 library for Arduino

Open the Arduino IDE and go to Sketch > Include Library > Manage Libraries.
Search for "MFRC522" and install the library by Miguel Balboa.



Connect the PIR motion sensor to the Arduino board

Connect the VCC pin of the PIR sensor to the 5V pin on the Arduino board.
Connect the GND pin of the PIR sensor to the GND pin on the Arduino board.
Connect the OUT pin of the PIR sensor to the digital pin 2 on the Arduino board.



Connect the servo motor and LED to the Arduino board

Connect the servo motor signal pin (usually orange or yellow) to the digital pin 9 on the Arduino board.
Connect the servo motor VCC pin (usually red) to the 5V pin on the Arduino board.
Connect the servo motor GND pin (usually brown or black) to the GND pin on the Arduino board.
Connect the anode (long leg) of the LED to the digital pin 13 on the Arduino board.
Connect the cathode (short leg) of the LED to the GND pin on the Arduino board through a 220-ohm resistor.



Connect the buzzer to the Arduino board

Connect the positive pin of the buzzer to the digital pin 3 on the Arduino board.
Connect the negative pin of the buzzer to the GND pin on the Arduino board.
